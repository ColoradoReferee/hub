FITNESS TEST
The FIFA fitness test for referees: six sprints, then forty runs. Colorado uses two sets of times: the FIFA men's international standard for the National test and the FIFA women's international standard for the Regional test.

TEST 1: REPEATED SPRINT ABILITY (RSA)
6 × 40 m, maximum 60 seconds recovery

Timing gates at 0 and 40 m; the start line is 1.5 m before the first gate. Walk back to the start during the recovery. Every sprint must be at or under the reference time.

One missed sprint earns a 7th

A referee who misses the time on one of the six runs a 7th sprint straight after the 6th. Miss two and the test is failed. A fall or a trip earns an extra trial, not a fail.

Colorado reference times

National test: 6.0 s (FIFA men's international). Regional test: 6.4 s men, 6.6 s women.

TEST 2: INTERVAL TEST
On a field instead of a track: the 75 m run goes from one penalty area line to the other, and the 25 m walk is split into 12.5 m at each end, turning around before the next run. Same times, same count.
40 × 75 m run, 25 m walk

Ten laps of a 400 m track. An audio signal starts each run and marks the end of each run and walk. Every run ends inside the walking area: a line 1.5 m before and 1.5 m after the 75 m mark.

One warning, then out

A foot late into the walking area once earns a clear warning. A second time and the test leader stops the referee: the test is failed.

Colorado reference times

National test: 15 s run, 18 s walk (FIFA men's international). Regional test: 17 s run; 20 s walk men, 24 s walk women.

Between the tests

Test 1 to Test 2: 6 to 8 minutes. Warm up before Test 1 as you would for a match.

ASSISTANT REFEREES
The AR test has three parts. Colorado runs the referee test above for everyone; this is here for referees heading to National AR or FIFA.
Test 1: CODA (change of direction)

10 m forward, 8 m sideways left, 8 m sideways right, 10 m forward. International reference: men 10.0 s, women 11.0 s. Two failed trials is a fail.

Test 2: RSA, 5 × 30 m

Maximum 30 seconds recovery. International reference: men 4.7 s, women 5.1 s. One miss earns a 6th trial; two misses is a fail.

Test 3: Interval, 40 × 75 m run and 25 m walk

Same track as the referees. International reference: men 15 s run, 20 s walk; women 17 s run, 22 s walk.
