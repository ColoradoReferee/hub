U9 AND U10: THE MODIFIED RULES
Build-out line at the thirds.

When the keeper has the ball in hand or on a goal kick, opponents retreat behind the line. They may cross once the keeper releases the ball.

  • No punts and no drop-kicks, even after a bounce. Indirect free kick to the opponents at the spot; inside the goal area, take it on the goal-area line parallel to the goal line.
  • Offside only past the build-out line. Between the halfway line and the build-out line there is no offside.
  • No deliberate heading. Indirect free kick at the spot; a defender's header in their own penalty area moves the kick to the goal-area line. Accidental contact with the head: play on.
  • Goal kicks: opponents behind the build-out line. Corner kicks: defenders 8 yards.
ALL AGES
  • Throw-ins: both hands, from behind the head, both feet down. A foul throw goes over to the opponents; coach the player as you call it.
  • Mismatch rule: a team down by 5 or more goals may add a player. The extra player comes off when the gap is back to 3.
  • Equipment: shin guards under socks; no jewelry or hard hair clips; taped medical or religious items are fine. Casts and braces only if you and both coaches agree they are appropriately padded. No metal or sharp cleats.
  • Uniforms: home wears dark, away wears light.
  • Sidelines: both teams on one side, spectators on the other. Coaches enter the field for injuries only, with your permission.
FIELD AND GOALS
  • U9-U10: 60 × 40 yards, build-out lines at the thirds, goals 6.5 × 18.5 ft, size 4 ball.
  • U11-U15: 75-80 × 50-55 yards, full markings, goals 6.5 × 18.5 or 7 × 21 ft. Size 4 ball through U12, size 5 from U13.
  • Corner kicks: defenders 8 yards at U9-U10, 10 yards at U11 and up. Goal kicks at U11+: opponents outside the penalty area.
