BEFORE KICKOFF
Check in every player and staff member with the Match Card, with photos.

Sign the roster and hand it back to the team. No player write-ins are permitted; a maximum of 18 players can participate.

  • Meet the Certified Athletic Trainer on site and introduce yourself before the game.
  • DPL Field Standards met: confirm and check off on the match card.
  • Match balls: the home team provides 3.
SUBSTITUTIONS
Five substitution moments per team per game.

Halftime is NOT a moment. Within a moment, substitutions and re-entry are unlimited at any stoppage, and a player can re-enter after being substituted at any time.

  • Temporary head injury substitution uses 1 substitution moment.
  • Substitution sheets MUST be used during all matches.
AFTER THE GAME
Fill out the standard match card

and give a copy to both teams.

Every red card needs a supplemental report.

Send it to the DPL league office at cameron@thedpl.com, the same day.
